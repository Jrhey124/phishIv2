How it works

Whitelist evidence, typosquatting evidence, and the character-level URL model are combined into one calibrated probability. A close match to a protected domain also raises a separate SUSPICIOUS warning when the probability is below the phishing threshold. Similarity alone does not prove a site is malicious.
